## 今日 10 词

### 1. realize
- **词性**：v.
- **意思**：意识到；明白
- **高频搭配**：`realize that...`
- **记忆钩子**：联想 `real + eyes`，真正“看见”了 → 意识到。
- **例句**：I realized that I was overthinking it.

### 2. assume
- **词性**：v.
- **意思**：以为；假设
- **高频搭配**：`assume that...`
- **记忆钩子**：先这么以为，但不一定是真的。
- **例句**：I assumed he was busy, so I didn't call.

### 3. handle
- **词性**：v.
- **意思**：处理；应对
- **高频搭配**：`handle a problem` / `handle pressure`
- **记忆钩子**：handle 也是“把手”；抓得住把手 → 事情处理得住。
- **例句**：I can handle it.

### 4. hesitate
- **词性**：v.
- **意思**：犹豫
- **高频搭配**：`hesitate to do sth`
- **记忆钩子**：读起来有一点停顿感，联想开口前犹豫。
- **例句**：Don't hesitate to ask me.

### 5. afford
- **词性**：v.
- **意思**：负担得起
- **高频搭配**：`afford sth` / `afford to do sth`
- **记忆钩子**：看到价格牌摸钱包：钱够不够？够 = can afford。
- **例句**：I can't afford to travel every month.

### 6. mention
- **词性**：v.
- **意思**：提到；顺便说起
- **高频搭配**：`mention sth` / `mention that...`
- **记忆钩子**：不是详细讲，而是顺嘴提一下。
- **例句**：She mentioned that she might move.

### 7. awkward
- **词性**：adj.
- **意思**：尴尬的；不自在的
- **高频搭配**：`feel awkward` / `awkward silence`
- **记忆钩子**：聊天突然冷场，空气很 awkward。
- **例句**：There was an awkward silence.

### 8. reliable
- **词性**：adj.
- **意思**：可靠的
- **高频搭配**：`a reliable person` / `a reliable service`
- **记忆钩子**：rely（依靠）+ able（能够）→ 能依靠的。
- **例句**：She's very reliable.

### 9. option
- **词性**：n.
- **意思**：选择；选项
- **高频搭配**：`another option` / `the best option`
- **记忆钩子**：想象菜单上的几个选择，每一个都是 an option。
- **例句**：We have two options.

### 10. perspective
- **词性**：n.
- **意思**：观点；视角
- **高频搭配**：`from my perspective`
- **记忆钩子**：你从哪个角度看问题，就是你的 perspective。
- **例句**：From my perspective, the problem is communication.

## 今天必须练熟的 5 句

1. **I realized that...**
2. **I assumed that...**
3. **I can handle it.**
4. **Don't hesitate to...**
5. **From my perspective...**

## Deep Talk 输出

### 题目
**Why do you like living in Thailand, and has living there changed the way you think about life?**

### 参考结构

> From my perspective, life here is more relaxed. I realized that I don't enjoy living under too much pressure. I used to assume that a busy life meant a successful life, but now I see it differently.

不要背整段。只需要抓住三个骨架：

- From my perspective...
- I realized that...
- I used to assume that...

然后换成你自己的真实内容。

## 2 秒主动回忆

只看中文，2 秒内说出英文：

- 意识到 → **realize**
- 以为 / 假设 → **assume**
- 处理 / 应对 → **handle**
- 犹豫 → **hesitate**
- 负担得起 → **afford**
- 提到 → **mention**
- 尴尬的 → **awkward**
- 可靠的 → **reliable**
- 选择 → **option**
